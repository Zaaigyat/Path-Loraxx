PRACTICAL TOOL 02

Find suitable job opportunities

Search by role, skill, or career interest. These are trusted starting points, not live vacancy listings. Check each opening's date, eligibility, location, and official application instructions.

Before applying: Confirm that the vacancy is still open, the employer is real, the duties are clear, and you meet the age and qualification requirements. Students should check applicable school rules and Philippine labor requirements.
